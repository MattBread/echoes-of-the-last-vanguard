The story

The world forgot.
Something remembers.

A decade after civilisation fell, the Vanguard protects what remains of humanity from the horrors beyond its walls. Matt joins their ranks searching for the truth behind his sister's disappearance. But when an ancient Grimoire recognises him, the past begins speaking — and the truth may be more terrifying than anything waiting in the darkness.
