01 / BIOGRAPHY

The strength to protect.

Tabitha is one of the central figures within the Vanguard's emerging team. Courageous, loyal and fiercely protective, she has built her life around ensuring that the people beside her do not have to face the darkness alone.


Her connection to Soul Magic gives her the ability to protect others, bind supernatural entities and channel spiritual energy. But every use of her power carries a personal cost.


Her history and the events that led her to the Written Arts will be uncovered gradually throughout the series.

02 / PERSONALITY

Fearless, loyal and compassionate.

Tabitha is confident, witty, tactical and courageous. She is protective of her friends and does not hesitate to put herself in danger when someone needs help.


She has a close friendship with Matt. They trust one another completely, share leadership of the central team and fight as exceptionally effective partners. Their relationship is a deep platonic friendship, never a romantic one.

03 / THE WRITTEN ARTS

Soul Magic

Tabitha's magic draws on spiritual energy and life force. It allows her to create protective barriers, bind supernatural beings and transfer vitality. Her fighting style combines close-range katana combat with powerful defensive and binding techniques.

Soul Shield

Creates a protective barrier using spiritual energy to defend herself and others.

Binding Chains

Summons supernatural chains capable of restraining dangerous entities.

Vitality Transfer

Transfers some of her own life energy to help another person survive.

Spirit Blade

Channels Soul energy through her katana to strike supernatural targets.

Sanctuary

Creates a protective area that shields nearby allies.

The Cost

Using her abilities consumes her own life energy, leaving her exhausted and vulnerable.

THE PRICE OF POWER
Every act of protection takes something from Tabitha. The more she gives, the more vulnerable she becomes.

04 / PERSONAL GRIMOIRE

The Book of the Bound Soul

✧

Tabitha's Grimoire contains the inscriptions that allow her to wield Soul Magic. Its pages hold knowledge about spiritual energy, binding and the connection between living beings.


Like every Grimoire, it is more than an ordinary book. Its full history and personality remain to be discovered.

05 / COMBAT & PARTNERSHIP

Stronger together.

Tabitha is a skilled close-range fighter who combines Japanese katana techniques with Soul Magic. She can defend allies, restrain enemies and create openings for devastating combined attacks.


Her partnership with Matt is central to the series. Together they develop a powerful combined technique known as Eclipse of the Fallen, combining Echo Magic and Soul Magic. The technique will be introduced later in the story when the stakes demand it.


Matt and Tabitha are equal co-leaders of their team. Their strengths complement each other, and their trust is one of the team's foundations.
