01 / BIOGRAPHY

A past that refuses to stay buried.

Matt grew up in a fortified settlement in the years following the Collapse. His life changed when his younger sister Mara disappeared at seventeen during the evacuation of their residential district. The Vanguard classified her disappearance as an unfortunate consequence of the evacuation. No body was recovered. No survivors came forward.


Matt never accepted the explanation. He joined the Vanguard to gain access to information that ordinary people could never see. But during his first deployment, an impossible radio transmission carries a familiar voice: his sister's.


As he investigates the mystery, Matt discovers that his own memories may be less reliable than he believes. Even his recruitment records contain a photograph of him in a Vanguard uniform years before he officially enlisted.

02 / PERSONALITY

Kindness in a broken world.

Matt is compassionate, observant, empathetic and quietly humorous. He is courageous without being reckless and has a strong instinct to protect the people around him. He often notices details others overlook.


As the story progresses, the voices and memories connected to his magic begin to affect his grip on reality. He does not become evil. Instead, he becomes increasingly desperate to understand what is happening and protect the people he cares about.

03 / THE WRITTEN ARTS

Echo Magic

Matt's magic allows him to perceive and interact with echoes left behind by memories, sounds and past events. His abilities make him particularly valuable when investigating places touched by supernatural activity.

Echo Sight

Perceives traces of past events and memories left behind in a location.

Phantom Strike

Channels Echo energy through his katana to strike supernatural targets.

Memory Trace

Follows fragments of memories connected to people or objects.

Silent Corridor

Creates a temporary space in which surrounding sound and perception become distorted.

Requiem

A powerful Echo technique whose full potential and consequences remain unknown.

The Cost

Repeated use erodes the reliability of his own memories and sense of reality.

THE PRICE OF POWER
Matt may forget familiar faces, confuse memories with reality or hear voices that no one else can hear. His greatest enemy may eventually become his own memory.

04 / PERSONAL GRIMOIRE

The Book of Forgotten Voices

✦

Matt's Grimoire contains a mysterious fragment of the ancient intelligence known as the Hollow One. Unlike an ordinary book, it appears to recognise Matt and possesses knowledge of events that have not yet happened.


Its pages whisper, conceal inscriptions and reveal messages that seem personally intended for him.

05 / COMBAT & RELATIONSHIPS

A partner, not a lone hero.

Matt fights with a Japanese katana, combining careful observation and Echo perception with close-range swordsmanship. He is not invincible, and his magic carries serious consequences.


He shares leadership of the central team with Tabitha. The two are very close friends and trusted combat partners. Their teamwork is built on mutual trust, loyalty and years of friendship.
